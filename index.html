Map layers

Every area you view online is cached automatically and shows again with no internet. To prepare a region in advance, zoom to it, pick a layer and press the button (up to 1500 tiles per press).

Put raster tiles in ./tiles/{z}/{x}/{y}.png (XYZ), e.g. mb-util india.mbtiles tiles, then serve this folder with npx serve. Without tiles you still get a graticule and reference cities. Map zoom 3–12.
